About Us

Sue, Paul & Abi. Born and bred in Yorkshire.

Sue started out her working life in banking, followed by stints in law and then Pest Control. Paul (originally a farmer) has ventured into the world of grain trading but still has a pedigree herd of Long Horn Cattle keeping in with the farming tradit.

Abi spent her final studies at Newcastle University studying Agricultural Business Management, graduating in 2025, she is now travelling Australia, New Zealand and the Far East.

We have a real passion for the countryside, rural life and our animals. Guests will meet Rhubarb and Revel the horses alongside Bella the pig (who has a racing car named after her).

Our cattle spend their days in the back field, if stopping in The Granary or The Stables you'll have a perfect view of them.

Hattie, Nell and Margot, our family dogs provide us with much entertainment. We also have a few chickens, a duck and a goose.

I am sure that you will come across some of our extended family during your stay at Birdforth Hall.

Old Black Bull, Raskelf

A small traditional village pub run by the Hannah family, situated in the beautiful village of Raskelf, North Yorkshire (just off the A19 north of York). A free house, the local beers are Theakston's Best & Timothy Taylors Landlord along with a regular choice of rotating guest ales.

The menu is traditional, fresh and ethical, with all produce supplied daily from the local farms & suppliers throughout Yorkshire.

FAQ's

Here are a few questions that you may have about your wedding day and the planning leading up to it. If there is anything else, then please just ask.

Should we take out wedding insurance?

It your choice, but we would suggest you to take it at the right time for future!

Will we have exclusive use of the venue?

Yes, you and your guests will have full exclusive use of the venue during your booking.

Do you allow dogs?

Yes, we are dog-friendly! you can check our managment work!

Will we be able to meet to discuss the wedding?

Absolutely! We arrange meeting and plannings before the wedding so taht everything would go smoothly.

Am I able to bring family and suppliers to visit the venue?

Yes, you are welcome to bring family members and supplier to go through to this place.

Will there be someone available on the day if we have any questions?

Our venue coordinator and staff will be available throughout your event day to assist you.

Is there on site parking?

Yes, parking is available for all hosts and guests.

Is The Forge suitable for disabled/wheelchair users?

Yes, The Forge is on the groundfloor , which is totally available for the disabled people.
